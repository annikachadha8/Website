About

I'm a junior at duPont Manual High School studying mechanical and chemical engineering. My work centers on designing assistive devices that expand mobility and independence for elderly users and people with mobility limitations.

Through my research at MIT's Research Science Institute and independent projects at LVL1 Makerspace, I've developed prototypes including the OmniWheel Walker, VitaSeat, and MagGRIP. I hold a provisional patent and have won multiple grand awards at Regeneron ISEF.

Beyond research, I founded Step Forward 501(c)(3), a nonprofit conducting early education outreach, and Hack the Track, Kentucky's first student-run hackathon. I'm passionate about building equitable access to STEM education.

Research & Innovation

OmniWheel Walker
2022–2024 U.S. Provisional Patent No. 63/699,222
Omnidirectional wheel system improving lateral stability for walker users. Drafted clinical trial protocol. Regeneron ISEF Grand Award, 3rd Place (2024).
VitaSeat: Smart Toilet Seat for Safe Transfer
2024–2025
Smart toilet seat with audio and haptic feedback for safe, independent transfers. Regeneron ISEF Special Award (2025).
MagGRIP: Magnetorheological Elastomer Traction Platform
2024–Present
Novel magnetic composite for slip-resistant assistive-device interfaces. Generalized across cane-tip and prosthetic-foot geometries. Regeneron ISEF Grand Award, 3rd Place (2026).
